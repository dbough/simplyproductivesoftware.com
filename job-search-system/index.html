Job Search System for Claude Beta

A plugin that builds your career profile, drafts a tailored resume and cover letter for a job you choose, checks the draft against facts you confirmed, and keeps a tracker of your applications in your own Google Drive.

These instructions were written for version 0.2.4.

What it does and does not do

  • You give it a job posting. It drafts a resume and cover letter from facts you have confirmed.
  • It never submits an application, sends an email or fills in a form. You edit the drafts and apply yourself.
  • It does not tell you whether a job is right for you. You choose the jobs.
  • Your information stays in your own Google Drive. Nothing personal is stored in the plugin.

Before you start

  • A Claude account. A free account worked in testing.
  • The Google Drive and Google Sheets connectors turned on in Claude. The plugin creates folders and a tracker sheet in your Drive, so it cannot work without them.
  • Your resumes or career notes, and about an hour for setup. You can stop after any step and pick up later.

Install

  1. In Claude on the web or the desktop app, open Customize, then Plugins, then Add, then Add marketplace.
  2. Enter Simply-Productive-Software/simply-productive-software. You do not need a GitHub account.
  3. Find Job Search System in the list and install it.

Using Claude Code instead? Run these two commands:

/plugin marketplace add Simply-Productive-Software/simply-productive-software
/plugin install job-search-system@simply-productive-software

Set it up

  1. Start a new chat and say: Set up the job search system.
  2. Claude shows the terms and asks you to confirm you understand them before it creates anything.
  3. It creates a folder in your Google Drive with a Profile folder, an Applications folder and a tracker sheet, then walks you through your career facts, what you want, your writing voice and your output settings.
  4. You confirm each fact. Drafts use only facts you have confirmed.

Use it

  1. Paste a job posting or link and ask for a resume and cover letter.
  2. Claude drafts them, has the draft checked, and saves the files to your Applications folder. The job gets a row in your tracker.
  3. Read the drafts and edit them. Check every claim before you send anything.
  4. Apply yourself on the employer's site. Then tell Claude you applied, skipped the job or parked it, and it updates the tracker.

Updates

The plugin does not always update on its own. To get a new version, open Customize, then Plugins, then Add, then Manage marketplaces, open the menu on the marketplace and choose Check for updates.

Good to know

  • This is an early beta. Things may change or break.
  • It is provided as is, and you use it at your own risk. AI output can be wrong, so check everything it writes.
  • You can use it free for your own job search. The license does not allow copying, redistributing or selling it.
